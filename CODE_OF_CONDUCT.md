Frostcap is built by volunteers. We want everyone who uses it, reports issues, reviews or
writes code to be able to take part without harassment or hostility.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first language is
  not English.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree with specifics and reasons.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and design, and that "no" or "not now"
  is a valid answer.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's personal information, and do not share
  recordings that show someone else's private data.
- Give credit, and only submit work you have the right to contribute.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, intimidation or stalking, in public or private channels.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, body, abilities
  or beliefs.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, spamming, or repeatedly pushing a topic after being
  asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information or communications without permission.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and any
chat or event run for the project. It also applies when someone represents the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers. Until a dedicated address is published here,
use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or contact
a maintainer directly, and say it is a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers decide on a response in proportion to what happened, from a private note, to a
public warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. They may remove
comments, commits, issues or other contributions that break this code. A maintainer involved
in a report does not take part in deciding on it.
